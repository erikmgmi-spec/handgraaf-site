Scope

Not every estate is a house

A principal's holdings rarely stop at the front door. The record is built to the assets you actually own — and each one carries the same maintenance intervals, vendor accountability and cost history as the residences.

Real estate

Primary, secondary and investment properties, domestic and abroad — each with its own zones, systems and staff.

Aviation

Aircraft coordinated and documented alongside the rest of the portfolio — inspection intervals, service records and vendor paperwork kept in one place.

Marine

Vessels documented with the same rigour as a residence — haul-outs, bottom work, crew coordination and yard invoices logged and tracked.

Automotive & collections

Collection vehicles, daily fleet, art and valuables — service history, appraised value and condition on record.

We work alongside aviation managers, brokers, marine crews and yards. We do not represent ourselves as aviation or marine specialists.
Scope is agreed per principal. An owner with one residence is not sold the same engagement as one with four homes, an aircraft and a vessel — and neither pays for the other's complexity.

Six / Control

Delegation is not surrender

The objection we hear most is that handing over the house means losing hold of it. The opposite is true: most owners have less control before a manager, not more, because nothing is written down.

01

You set the threshold. Work below an agreed figure proceeds. Above it, nothing moves without your approval.

02

Vendor credentials, licensing and insurance are documented where applicable to the work and engagement. The record shows what was quoted, what was done, what was paid, and who authorised it.

03

The professional may change. The estate does not start over. Nothing load-bearing lives in one person's head — it is recorded in the Keep, and the Keep stays with the house.

04

You can audit any line, any time. Not on request, not next quarter. Open the tab.

05

Insurance requirements are established according to the engagement and role. Where applicable, evidence of coverage and additional-insured status is documented before access is granted.

Seven / Discretion

Your records are yours alone

Each estate is a separate, isolated database. No owner's records touch another's, and there is no shared directory of clients.

Access is granted to named accounts only, enforced at the database itself — not merely hidden behind a login screen.

Access is revocable at any time, by you. Should our engagement end, the record of your home remains with your home.

The Keep runs on Google Cloud and Netlify, which hold their own independent certifications. Those certifications belong to the infrastructure providers, not to Handgraaf — we make no claim to be certified ourselves, and would rather say so than let the logos imply otherwise.

Continuity

Estate software is bought once and then ages. Ours is maintained continuously, because we operate it every day on live estates — when something is missing, it is built, and every principal benefits from it.

Plainly

What estate management actually is

The term is used loosely, and it is used to mean three different things. Here is how we use it.

Estate management

Running a private residence and everything attached to it on the owner's behalf: staff, vendors, maintenance, projects, budgets and records. The manager works for the household, not for a building or a landlord.

Property management

Administering a property as an investment. Rent collection, tenants, leases and compliance. The client is an investor, the occupant is a tenant, and the measure is yield.

Household staffing

Finding, assessing and placing the people who work in a private home. A conventional placement may conclude after the hire; Handgraaf can continue through household integration, standards implementation and the permanent operating record.

Handgraaf Estates is built around the first and the third, and the record is what joins them. We support an owner's investment property when it is asked of us — tenant coordination included — but that is not our focus, and we do not run portfolios at scale.
Questions

What owners ask before they start

Do I have to replace the staff I already have?
No. Existing staff and vendors are retained unless you ask otherwise. Intake catalogues who already works at the property and what they are responsible for, and they continue inside the record rather than outside it.
What does estate management cost?
It is bespoke — priced to your request and the intensity of scope, because an owner with one residence is not buying the same engagement as one with four homes and a vessel. Cost is driven by property count, staff headcount, asset classes and travel. We quote after the walkthrough, not before.
How is this different from estate management software?
Software is sold to you and then operated by you. The system here is operated by the manager who runs your estate, and it is populated as the work happens rather than by you entering data. You receive the record; you are not asked to maintain it.
Who can see my records?
Only named accounts you approve. Each estate is a separate, isolated database enforced at the database itself, not hidden behind a shared login. No owner's records touch another's, and there is no shared client directory.
What happens if we stop working together?
The record of your home stays with your home. Access is revocable by you at any time, and the estate does not restart from zero if the manager changes — including if the manager is us.
Can I speak with a current client before I commit?
We don't publish a client list, but we're glad to arrange a private reference conversation with a current principal, with their permission. Discretion protects our clients from being named publicly — it was never meant to mean you have to take our word for it.
Do you manage homes outside San Diego?
Yes. Out-of-state and overseas residences run the identical workflow and appear on the same calendar in the same format. Our household staffing division also places nationally, with placements made in Miami, Atherton and Los Angeles.
Is AI making decisions about my property?
It documents, it does not decide. An automated review agent fills in facts already evidenced in your record — a model number, a warranty date, an equipment link — and flags anything that would need a vendor or a purchase for your manager to review first. It never contacts anyone, never authorizes spending, and every entry it makes is traceable to its source record. Your data is never used as training material.
