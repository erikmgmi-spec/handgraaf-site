Control

Delegation is not surrender

Most owners have less control before a manager, not more, because nothing is written down.

01

You set the threshold. Below an agreed figure, work proceeds. Above it, nothing moves without your approval.

02

Vendor proof comes first. Requirements depend on the work and engagement; where applicable, licensing and insurance are documented before access is granted.

03

Audit any line, any time. The record shows what was quoted, done and paid, and who authorised it.

04

Documentation

The record writes itself, where it safely can

We operate the Keep daily on live estates, so it improves continuously, without a migration. An automated review agent documents facts already in your record, such as a model or warranty date, and flags anything needing a vendor or spending for your manager.

Discretion

Your records are yours alone

Each estate is a separate, isolated database. There is no shared client directory.

Access is limited to named accounts, enforced at the database itself, not just a login screen.

You can revoke access at any time. If our engagement ends, the record stays with your home.

The Keep runs on Google Cloud and Netlify. Their certifications are theirs; we claim none of our own.

Questions

What owners ask before they start

Do I have to replace my current staff?
No. Existing staff and vendors are retained unless you ask otherwise, and continue inside the record.
What does it cost?
It is priced to scope: property count, staff headcount, asset classes and travel. We quote after the walkthrough, not before.
Can I speak with a current client?
We don’t publish a client list, but we can arrange a private reference conversation with a current principal, with their permission.
Do you manage homes outside San Diego?
Yes. Out-of-state and overseas residences run the same workflow, on the same calendar.
What happens if we stop working together?
The record stays with your home. The estate does not restart from zero if the manager changes, including if the manager is us.
Is AI making decisions about my property?
No. It documents facts already in your record and flags the rest for your manager. It never contacts anyone or authorizes spending.
